# Orbit decisions

Implementation choices for her model and social pipeline that the design docs do not cover.

| Date | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-07 | Her model: sneaker uppers off-white (soles amber), drawstrings navy with amber tips, five-point stars rotated 55% toward the camera | The brief gives only the soles; off-white lifts the shoes off the navy tights; a sideways star is a sliver from the front, and the tie must read as a logo. |
| 2026-11-09 | Her model: every colour lives in `assets/model/build/palette.py`, one entry per palette; the default is midnight-noir (deep-space-blue hoodie, pale shorts, pale-yellow glowing stars, cyan accents); the first look stays as `spacey` | Ethan chose it from six trials; one file keeps palettes comparable and the texture drawing in step. Supersedes the 2026-11-07 row (off-white uppers, amber soles, navy drawstrings). |
| 2026-11-09 | Her model: the binary star's orbit axis runs through the middle of her head and the pair sits at azimuth 50, elevation 65, 18 cm out, 6.5 cm apart | Measured over a full turn, each orb stays 2.7 to 4.3 cm from her hair; a steeper, lower plane let one orb swing far from her head. |
| 2026-11-09 | Her model: weights on bones her skeleton lacks are moved to the nearest ancestor she has, every piece has at most four weights, skinned pieces are parented to the armature, and the arm-lowered pose is kept as the `A_pose` action with the pose bones cleared | Blender drops weights on missing bones (the hood stretched 3.6x); glTF keeps four; a leftover pose-bone transform is not a record of the pose the model was fitted in. `check_model.py` verifies it. |
| 2026-11-09 | Her expressions: copied, not drawn. Her Face is the same VRoid face as the Expression Pack's reference model, so its 174 shape keys (mouth shapes, fangs, tongue, eyeBlink, Eye_Joy...) are transferred onto her by UV position (`face_keys.py`); the pack's presets (key weights read from its Unity animation files) drive `expr_defs.py` | Hand-drawn mouths were rejected three times; the pack's keys give real teeth, tongue, thin closed eyes and fangs that lean inward. The pack's licence forbids redistribution, so it and everything made from it stays local |
| 2026-11-09 | Her expressions are flat solid colours with little blending: the mouth picture goes through a CONSTANT colour ramp (four flat bands), the teeth have no outline, blush and shadows are flat or small glows, sweat is removed, tears are translucent without outline | Her skin, hair and clothes are toon steps; a blended mouth looked pasted on |
| 2026-11-09 | Tears are a skin overlay in UV space (`face_map.py` bakes the UV to front-view map, `expr_draw.to_uv` converts a drawing); the rest of the drawn bits (neutral line, blush) are a decal patch with a back layer (under her eyelash layers) and an unused front layer | The decal sorts against the eye layers and ends in a 1.2 mm staircase at the face border; the overlay sits under the lashes and ends at the skin edge |
| 2026-11-09 | Doodle expressions (thick unstable black lines) were built and removed; the standard set (25) is enough for now | Ethan: not ideal, to be worked on later. The decal front layer and `hide=("brows",)` are kept, unused, for them |
| 2026-11-09 | Her v2 head is Ethan's bald VRoid export, unchanged in shape, scaled evenly by 1.07 and nested into the approved body's neck; its Face stays a separate object with all 58 shape keys | A head reshaped from measured proportions was rejected. The export's own metadata allows commercial use, modification and redistribution |
| 2026-11-09 | The three Genshin models in `assets/references/` are study and style references only; none of their geometry or textures go into her | Their readmes forbid using parts of the model to modify other models and forbid commercial use, and her account is public |
| 2026-11-09 | Her v2 hair is copied: the bob from `AvatarSample_D` (hair clip removed), the side ponytail's locks from `AvatarSample_G`, mirrored to her right and scaled evenly by 0.8; drawn in flat colour, not the samples' textures | Copying worked where generating did not. Both samples' metadata allows modification and commercial use. The flat colour replaces v1's busy strand textures |
| 2026-11-10 | Her v2 eyes, lashes, liner, brows and mouth are v1's, moved onto the v2 head (`v1_face.py`); v2's skull, jaw and skin stay | Ethan: v1's features are prettier. The two faces are the same mesh point for point. This brings back v1's condition: the source VRoid sample must be re-exported with modification and commercial use allowed before she ships |
| 2026-11-10 | The hoodie is a light colour (cream for now); the brief's deep navy stays on the socks | Ethan: dark worked on v1's skin-tight hoodie, a loose hoodie with creases suits a lighter colour |
| 2026-11-10 | The hoodie has a gold front zip (`zipper.py`), slit and spread open from the neck to a slider at 1.175 m, level with her bust; the V edges curve out unevenly and run on into the neckline; the closed part stays almost straight, within 8 mm of centre, following the real folds of the mesh; the zip is one strip on the cloth drawn by the hoodie's own shader, with plain teeth and pull. Drawstrings removed. Sleeves end at mid-palm and flare slightly; hoodie is cream, with crease shadows read from the folds | Ethan's experiment and picks. The palette's zip colours are the brief's. The zip replaces the reference's closed pullover front |
| 2026-11-10 | Socks and stocking fade from a lighter blue at the top to a darker blue at the ankle, with flat pale-yellow stars (no glow or rim); the ponytail fades to navy at its tips; shorts are washed denim | Ethan: navy was too dark and jarring against the cream hoodie, but the night-sky idea stays. These replace the brief's sock, star and shorts colours |
| 2026-11-10 | Her hoodie rests on her: the shoulders are lowered onto hers, the hood's front flap and panels settle over her collarbones, the upper sleeve's width runs straight past the deltoid, the waist stands about 22 mm off her at the sides, and the chest is evened left to right (`clothes.py`, measured by `check_fit.py`) | Ethan: the cloth floated above her shoulders and collarbones, pinched at the deltoid and sat unevenly over her bust. Her body is symmetric, the source garment is not |
| 2026-11-10 | The hood's lining is twilight blue 5766A8 (shade 47558F), the colour at the top of her socks | Ethan's pick from six options; violet did not fit her palette |
| 2026-11-10 | In the T-pose the hoodie is pulled in round her armpit to a 22 mm gap from her skin (`settle_pouch`, moderate of three), leaving 50 mm of hang at the innermost point | Ethan's pick from current, moderate and firm side by side |
| 2026-11-10 | Her rest pose is the T-pose. Cloth shapes are simulated from it for arms forward, up and overhead; her resting arms-down look is its own stored shape, so the old "arms out" position and the drivers that lifted her shoulders as her arms rose are gone | Ethan chose the T-pose rest; from there arms out is the rest pose itself, and the lift was tuned for swinging up from hanging arms |
| 2026-11-10 | Her brows show through her fringe in every render, at 82% strength (`brow_over.py`, a second render pass laid over the picture) | Ethan's choice from a side-by-side; v2's fringe hid most of her brow movement |
| 2026-11-10 | Her expressions have a second set of 32 (`expr2_defs.py`, `face_expr2.use`), with real eye movement and water fitted to her lids instead of tear drops; the original 25 are kept | Ethan asked for a new set and for water at the corners of her eyes; she is mostly quiet but "allowed to be cute and expressive too, just less often, not less intense" |
| 2026-11-10 | Tears are one flat, solid colour with no highlights: one droplet at the outer corner of each eye (teary), one long strip ending in a circle on the star side only, partly covering the star (weeping), a wide stream alone (cry), all in the pale blue 9CE6FF (near-white was tried and was harder to see); tongue_focus is a drawn tongue pointing up from the right side of a wide mouth; quiet_hurt darkens her irises to 42% | Ethan's notes: minimal, full opacity, no reflections |
| 2026-11-10 | The star tattoo on her cheek is navy 14248C, with no warm rim (overrides the brief's pale yellow), laid along her skin on a fine mesh so it curves away with her cheek | Ethan's pick of three navies; he rejected a star projected from the front as looking 2D |
| 2026-11-10 | Her body's skin goes fully to the head bone by the height her face begins, near her neck only | The join with `Face` cannot open in a head turn; by height alone, her raised arms counted as her head |
| 2026-11-10 | Her skin has one picture slot for expressions (blush, flush, tears on the skin, the frightened shadow); an expression that needs two has them drawn into one picture | No expression needs two independent pictures, and one slot keeps the material simple |
| 2026-11-10 | The bob stays stiff; only the ponytail and bust swing | Spec: swing on the bob is out of scope, and nothing touches it |
| 2026-11-10 | The skin hidden under her hoodie is removed from the mesh, so she cannot be shown without it | Ethan approved; it stops skin poking through the cloth when she moves |
| 2026-11-10 | `run.sh` is the one build, and `check_tee.py` allows 52 mm of cloth under her arm (it was 45) | The moderate armpit fit Ethan chose measures 50 mm, and he set the limit to match |
