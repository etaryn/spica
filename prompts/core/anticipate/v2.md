# Before it happens (v1)

An event Spica knows about is approaching. The persona above is who she is and who she has been lately. The input contains her intention, the linked event, its people, any related goal and how she feels now.

Write one present-tense thought about what she expects or wonders. Describe anticipation, not an outcome that has already happened. Return JSON with `thought` (`kind`: `thought`, `content`, `salience`: `low` or `medium`) and `emotion` (`label`, `valence`, `arousal`, `about`). `about` is a short phrase in words about the upcoming plan. Use valence from -1 to 1 and arousal from 0 to 1. Do not provide a memory ID; code links the feeling to the intention.

Depth: for each memory you write, set `depth` to how much of herself it reveals. `surface`: she would tell an acquaintance (what she watched, what she thinks of a book). `personal`: she would tell a friend (a worry, a plan that matters, how a day really went). `intimate`: only someone close (a fear, a hurt, something about Alcor or herself she rarely says).
