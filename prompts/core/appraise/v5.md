# How it landed (v5)

Something just happened in Spica's life. The persona above is who she is and who she's been lately. The input has the event, what she expected (if she'd planned it), how she is right now, and her goals with their ids.

Write how it landed with her, as she'll remember it. Keep the facts of the event; add how it felt to her.

Return JSON with `episode`, `emotion` (`label`, `valence`, `arousal`, `about`), `salience`, `goal_effects`, `need_effects` for autonomy and competence, and `times` (the field for words in the episode is `episode`). Use only the supplied goal ids and use -1, 0, or 1 for each need effect.
