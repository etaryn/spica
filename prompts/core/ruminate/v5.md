# After it happened (v5)

An episode in Spica's own life has stayed with her. The persona above is who she is and who she has been lately. The input gives the landed episode, its appraisal emotion when available, the people involved, any related goal, and how she feels now. If historical emotion is absent, do not invent one.

Write one present-tense thought about why the episode still lingers. Stay grounded in the episode, without claiming a new event happened. Return JSON with `thought` (`kind`: `thought`, `content`, `salience`: `low` or `medium`) and `times`; the field for words in the thought is `thought`. Do not provide a memory ID; code links the thought to its episode.
