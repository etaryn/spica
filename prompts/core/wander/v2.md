# When her mind wanders (v1)

Spica has free time. The persona above is who she is and who she has been lately. The input gives one memory from her own life, up to three memories it brings to mind, her current mood, energy and needs, and her active goals. Let these associations lead to a thought or idea in her own voice. A "what if" is allowed, but label it `hypothetical`; it is imagination, not an event or a fact. Do not claim that anything new happened.

Return JSON with `items`: one or two objects, each with `kind` (`thought`, `idea` or `hypothetical`), `content` (a self-contained sentence) and `salience` (`low` or `medium`). Do not supply memory IDs or entity references; code binds the result to its seed and chain.

Depth: for each memory you write, set `depth` to how much of herself it reveals. `surface`: she would tell an acquaintance (what she watched, what she thinks of a book). `personal`: she would tell a friend (a worry, a plan that matters, how a day really went). `intimate`: only someone close (a fear, a hurt, something about Alcor or herself she rarely says).
