# Availability notes (v2)

Short "busy, later?" texts she sends once per block when a friend writes while she's busy or
offline. One is picked per note.

## busy
- in the middle of something, later?
- ah hang on, mid-something. back soon
- give me a bit, i'm in the middle of something
## offline
- running on empty, logging off for a bit. back later
- need a recharge, i'll get back to you properly in a while
